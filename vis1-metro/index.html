    Encoding, interaction & data notes

    Encoding

    • Position (x): station order along each line, evenly spaced (schematic, not geographic).
    • Position (y), above the line: price per m². Thin whisker = 10th–90th percentile; box = 25th–75th; white tick = median.
    • Texture: a hatched box means the typical sale is more than 2 km from its "nearest" station, i.e. not walkable.
    • Area of the station circle: number of sales (10% sample). Hollow tick = no sales attributed.
    • Length of the bar below the line: number of Foursquare venues around the station, split by colour into 5 venue groups.
    • Colour of the line: identity of the line (Red, Green, Tram).

    Interaction

    • Hover a station: tooltip with its key numbers.
    • Click a station: the panel shows its histogram vs Dubai, a waffle of unit types, a geographic map of the communities whose sales were attributed to it, and the top communities.
    • Drag on a price axis: brush a price band; stations whose median falls outside it fade on all three lines.
    • Period / registration buttons recompute every glyph.

    Data & cleaning

    • Residential sales (units and villas) from transactions.csv, 2015 to 17 Sep 2026, with the price and size bounds from our prep script (42,323 sales kept).
    • nearest_metro_en mixes metro stations, tram stops, renamed stations (Damac → R36, Sharaf DG → Mashreq, First Abu Dhabi Bank → FGB) and a typo ("Jumeirah Beach Resdency"). We mapped all 56 labels onto station codes from dubai_metro_stations.geojson and RTA's tram order.
    • 32% of all transactions have no nearest station; they are summarised in the box below the map.
    • Distance is measured from the DLD area centroid to the station, so it is approximate.
