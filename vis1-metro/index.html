    Encoding, interaction & data notes

    Encoding

    • Vertical position: station order along each line, like a line diagram on a platform wall (schematic, not geographic). Names are horizontal so they can be read.
    • Horizontal position of the dot: median price per m² of residential sales whose DLD "nearest station" is this stop, on one shared scale. The thick line behind it covers the middle half of sales (25th–75th percentile). The dashed line is Dubai's median.
    • Hollow dot: the typical sale is more than 2 km from the station, so it is not walkable.
    • Bar length: homes sold (10% sample), or annual metro riders from RTA, or Foursquare places nearby.
    • Line colour: Red Line, Green Line, Dubai Tram.

    Interaction

    • The four finding cards jump to the station that proves each point.
    • Hover a row for its numbers; click it to fill the profile panel (histogram vs Dubai, unit-type waffle, map of where its buyers' homes are, places nearby).
    • Drag across a price axis to highlight stations whose median falls in that range.
    • Buttons change the period, off-plan/ready, and what the bar shows.

    Data & cleaning

    • Clean residential sales (units and villas) from transactions.csv, 2015 to 17 Sep 2026.
    • All 56 labels of nearest_metro_en (metro stations, tram stops, renamed stations, a typo) were mapped to station codes.
    • Distance runs from the DLD area centroid to the station, so it is approximate.
    • Outside data: RTA annual riders for the 14 stations RTA publishes (2019, 2022–2025; Dubai Media Office), and the original freehold areas of Dubai Regulation No. 3 of 2006. All links: data/external/SOURCES.md.
