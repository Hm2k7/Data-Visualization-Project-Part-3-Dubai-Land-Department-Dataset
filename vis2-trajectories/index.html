Encoding, interaction & data notes

Encoding

  • Grid map: one square per community with at least 100 clean residential sales. Squares are placed in a 13x11 grid by a greedy assignment that keeps each community as close as possible to its true centroid (a small-multiples grid map, Meulemans et al. 2017).
  • Line inside the square: median AED/m² by year, 2015-2026, on one shared scale (0-40k) for all squares. The dot marks the year on the slider.
  • Square colour (diverging VSUP): orange = below the Dubai-wide value, blue = above, darker = further away. As the 90% bootstrap interval widens, the colour gets fewer steps and moves towards neutral grey, so unreliable squares read as "can't tell".
  • Connected scatterplot: x = sales per year (log scale, 10% sample), y = median AED/m², one dot per year joined in time order; colour = community identity (max 3), grey = all of Dubai.
  • Price line (below): the same communities as a plain line chart of median AED/m² by year, for readers new to connected scatterplots.

Interaction

  • Hover a square: name, DLD name, value with its 90% interval and sample size.
  • Click a square: add or remove it in the connected scatterplot (up to 3; the oldest drops out).
  • Year slider / Play: draws the paths up to that year and moves the year marker in every square, so both views stay in sync.
  • Switch the colour metric, or turn VSUP off to see how much the plain palette over-states small samples.

Data & cleaning

  • Clean residential sales (see prep script). A yearly median needs at least 5 sales, otherwise the year is left out.
  • Growth = median AED/m² 2024-26 / median 2018-20 - 1. Its interval comes from 1,000 bootstrap resamples of both periods.
  • Growth mixes real price change with a change in what is sold (for example, more luxury off-plan launches). So it shows how much more the typical m² sold for, which is not the same as a like-for-like price index.
  • 2026 volume is annualised (x1.404); prices are not.
  • Outside data, real prices: "Real (2015 AED)" divides every sale's price per m² by the UAE consumer price index of its year (World Bank, FP.CPI.TOTL.ZG, 2015 = 100; 2025 = 113.2). There is no 2026 figure yet, so 2026 uses the 2025 level. Medians, growth and bootstrap intervals are then recomputed on the deflated prices.
  • Names: DLD community names (e.g. Al Barsha South Fourth) are shown with their market name (JVC) from the dominant master_project_en.
