Encoding, interaction & data notes

Encoding

  • Row order: rank by weighted score (or by one criterion if you click its column header).
  • Stacked bar: total length = score (0-100); each coloured segment = weight x criterion score, so its length shows that criterion's contribution. Colour = criterion (8 fixed colours, same order everywhere).
  • Criterion columns: bar length = the community's percentile among the ranked communities (budget fit = raw share within budget). Hatched = unknown, counted as average.
  • Strongest reasons: the two criteria contributing most to the score (only if the community is in the top 40% on them), written in plain words.
  • Headline sentence: rewritten live from the current top 3 and budget.
  • ▲▼ beside the rank: how many places the row moved after your last change.
  • Map: numbered circles at community centroids (position), metro lines in their line colours.
  • Budget check: a unit dot plot. Each dot = 1 (or k) recent sales, x = price (log scale), colour = ready vs off-plan, vertical rule = your budget.

Interaction

  • Home type, budget and Ready/Off-plan recompute the live criteria from the 2024-26 sales.
  • Weight sliders and persona presets re-weight the scores; rows animate to their new rank.
  • Click a column header to rank by that criterion only; click "Score" to go back.
  • Hovering a row highlights its marker on the map, and hovering a marker highlights its row.
  • Click a row to open its budget check.

Data

  • Budget fit, Value (median AED/m²), Liquidity (number of sales), Ready share: clean residential sales 2024 to Sep 2026 of the chosen home type. Communities with fewer than 10 such sales (10% sample) are listed but not ranked.
  • Momentum: real growth of median AED/m², 2018-20 --> 2024-26 (all homes): each sale is first divided by the UAE consumer price index of its year (World Bank, outside data), so momentum is growth beyond inflation.
  • Metro access: straight-line km from the community centroid (dubai.geojson) to the nearest of the 56 stations in dubai_metro_stations.geojson.
  • Amenities: Foursquare venues in dubai_venues.csv. The file caps a community at 100 venues, so "100+" communities are tied at the top.
  • Established: residents per km² in 2019 (dubai.geojson population).
